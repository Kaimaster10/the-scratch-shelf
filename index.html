The Scratch Shelf is an unofficial fan project, not affiliated with the book, its publisher, Goodreads or Open Library. Your progress stays in this browser; use Export to keep a backup.

Share your shelf

Your link shows your name and which books you’ve read. Ratings, notes and dates stay private. Anyone with the link can see it. It’s a snapshot, so send a fresh link when you want friends to see your latest.

Add a friend

Tapping a friend’s link works too. If you use the home screen icon on an iPhone, paste the link here so it’s saved in the app.
